## 1. Who we are and what this covers

Dmytro Melnyk, Ukraine (“we”, “us”) makes TakeSync and is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact: [support@takesync.uk](mailto:support@takesync.uk).

This policy covers the TakeSync desktop application for macOS (the “App”), the website takesync.uk (the “Site”), purchases and support. It should be read together with the [TakeSync End User License Agreement](/terms).

**In short:** the App processes your audio and video only on your computer. We collect the minimum needed to sell and license the App and to answer your emails. We do not sell personal data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

## 2. The App

**Your media stays on your computer.** The App reads the audio and video files you add, analyses them, and writes timelines and files to folders you choose. It does not upload your media, its contents, your projects or your exports to us or to anyone else. Project data is stored in a local database on your computer.

**No telemetry.** The App does not send usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

**Optional FFmpeg download.** If you choose to install FFmpeg through the App, your computer downloads it directly from the third-party provider shown in the App (currently ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de). That provider receives your IP address and standard request data, as with any download. We receive nothing.

**License activation.** When you activate a purchased license, the App sends us or our licensing provider: your License Key, the email address and order number linked to it, an anonymous identifier of the computer (a one-way hash, not your files), the computer’s name as set in its system settings (so you can tell your computers apart), and the App version. We use this to check that the key is valid and to enforce the limit of 2 computers per license. The App repeats this check about once a week and keeps working offline between checks for up to 30 days.

**Trial.** When you start the free trial, the App sends us the same anonymous identifier of the computer, once, so the trial start is remembered for that computer and a reinstall does not start a new trial. We store only that identifier and the date.

## 3. Purchases

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com Market Limited (“Paddle”), the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle runs the checkout, takes payment, charges sales tax or VAT, issues invoices and handles refunds. For that, Paddle is an independent controller of the data you give at checkout, under [Paddle’s privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/privacy-buyers).

We never see or store your card or bank details. Paddle shares with us what we need to deliver and support your license: your name, email address, country, order number, product, price paid, and refund or chargeback status.

## 4. The Site

The Site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the Site from abuse, Cloudflare processes your IP address and standard request data (browser type, page requested, time) and may keep them in short-lived logs. See [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

- **No cookies or tracking.** The Site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers.
- **Fonts.** The Site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address under [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Local prices.** To show an approximate price in your currency, the Site may fetch today’s exchange rates from open.er-api.com. Your browser’s time zone and language are used for this in your browser only and are not sent to us.
- **Language.** Your choice of language may be remembered in your browser’s local storage. It stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
- **Downloads.** Installers are served by Cloudflare from takesync.uk.

## 5. Support emails

When you write to [support@takesync.uk](mailto:support@takesync.uk), we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to answer you and fix problems. Please do not send media files unless we ask; a description, screenshot or log usually helps more.

Email to that address is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox hosted by Google (Gmail). Both process the messages on our behalf.

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR / UK GDPR) | Kept for |

**Who else receives data.** Only the providers named in this policy: Paddle (payments), Cloudflare (Site, downloads, email forwarding and license checks), Brevo / Sendinblue SAS, France (sends the email with your License Key), Google (mailbox and fonts) and open.er-api.com (exchange rates). We do not sell or rent personal data, and we disclose it to authorities only when the law requires.

**International transfers.** These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where data leaves the EU or the UK, they rely on adequacy decisions or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

**Security.** Access to our accounts is protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication, and we keep only the data listed above.

## 7. Your rights and contact

You may ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit or object to its use, or give you a copy in a portable format. Write to [support@takesync.uk](mailto:support@takesync.uk); we answer within 30 days. We may need to keep purchase records that tax law requires even if you ask us to delete them. For data Paddle holds, you can also contact Paddle directly.

If you are in the EU or the UK and think we handle your data unlawfully, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

**Children.** TakeSync is a professional tool not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

**Changes.** If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new date, and tell licensed customers by email about significant changes.
