## 2. The App

**Your media stays on your computer.** The App reads the audio and video files you add, analyses them, and writes timelines and files to folders you choose. It does not upload your media, its contents, your projects or your exports to us or to anyone else. Project data is stored in a local database on your computer.

**No telemetry.** The App does not send usage statistics, analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers.

**Optional FFmpeg download.** If you choose to install FFmpeg through the App, your computer downloads it directly from the third-party provider shown in the App (currently ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de on macOS and github.com on Windows). That provider receives your IP address and standard request data, as with any download. We receive nothing.

**License activation.** When you activate a purchased license, the App sends us or our licensing provider: your License Key, the email address and order number linked to it, an anonymous identifier of the computer (not your name or files), the operating system, and the App version. We use this to check that the key is valid and to enforce the limit of 2 computers per license. The App may repeat this check from time to time; it works offline between checks.

**Premiere Pro panel.** The optional TakeSync panel for Adobe Premiere Pro reads exports the App saved on your computer and imports them into your open project. It sends nothing over the internet.

## 3. Purchases

TakeSync is sold by Paddle.com Market Limited (“Paddle”), which acts as merchant of record. Paddle runs the checkout, takes payment, charges sales tax or VAT, issues invoices and handles refunds. For that, Paddle is an independent controller of the data you give at checkout, under [Paddle’s privacy policy](https://www.paddle.com/privacy-buyers).

We never see or store your card or bank details. Paddle shares with us what we need to deliver and support your license: your name, email address, country, order number, product, price paid, and refund or chargeback status.

## 4. The Site

The Site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver pages and protect the Site from abuse, Cloudflare processes your IP address and standard request data (browser type, page requested, time) and may keep them in short-lived logs. See [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

- **No cookies or tracking.** The Site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. Fonts and scripts are served from the Site itself.
- **Language.** Your choice of English or Ukrainian may be remembered in your browser’s local storage. It stays in your browser and is not sent to us.
- **Downloads.** Installers are downloaded from GitHub, which receives your IP address and request data under [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 5. Support emails

When you write to [support@takesync.uk](mailto:support@takesync.uk), we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach, and use them only to answer you and fix problems. Please do not send media files unless we ask; a description, screenshot or log usually helps more.

Email to that address is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a mailbox hosted by Google (Gmail). Both process the messages on our behalf.
