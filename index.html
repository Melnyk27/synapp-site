      What it does for you

      Synced by sound

      Every clip is matched to the others by its audio. Any camera, phone, recorder or lav works — no timecode or slate needed.

      Nothing gets lost

      B-roll from a camera rolling on its own has no sound partner. TakeSync places it by the camera’s clock, at the time of day it was shot.

      False matches caught

      A song played twice or an interview over the ceremony can look like a match. Every match is checked against the device clocks, and the ones that can’t be true are dropped.

      The day, in order

      The timeline follows the real time of day: ceremony, portraits, speeches, first dance. Long stretches where nobody recorded are squeezed out.

      Recorder sound, swapped in

      One tick adds a second sequence where camera audio is replaced by the recorder or lav. Overlapping clips get their own tracks, so nothing hides under anything.

      Private and offline

      Your footage never leaves your Mac. No upload, no account, no internet needed to sync.

      Fast on any drive

      TakeSync checks whether your footage sits on an SSD, a spinning hard drive or a network share, and reads each the fastest way: every core on an SSD, two files at a time from an HDD, several drives at once. Footage it has already analysed is never decoded again.

      Simple, and it never freezes

      Three steps, one Sync button, nothing to configure. Analysis runs in the background, so the window stays responsive while TakeSync works.

      Questions

      Do I need timecode or a slate?

      No. TakeSync compares the sound recorded by different devices at the same time. Cameras without timecode and phones work fine.

      Which formats does it read?

      MP4, MOV, WAV, AIFF, M4A and MP3 out of the box. MXF, MTS and other formats work after you install FFmpeg from Settings.

      What happens to a clip with no sound partner?

      If its camera’s clock is reliable, the clip goes on the timeline at the time it was recorded and is marked so you can check it. Otherwise it’s marked as not synced, and on export you can move such clips to the end.

      Does it fix drift on long recordings?

      Yes. TakeSync detects when a recorder runs slightly fast or slow against the cameras and corrects the audio.

      What about Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Windows?

      Final Cut Pro and DaVinci Resolve export is being tested now. Windows is in development. They arrive as free updates.

      Monthly, yearly or lifetime — what’s the difference?

      Only how you pay. A subscription keeps TakeSync working while it’s active, and you can cancel any time. The lifetime licence is paid once and never expires. Every plan includes all features and updates.
