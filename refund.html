Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Full refund, for any reason, within 14 days. This applies to every TakeSync plan — monthly, yearly and one-time — and to every subscription renewal charge.

How to ask for a refund

Write to support@takesync.uk with your order number, or contact Paddle through the link in your receipt. You do not need to give a reason. The money goes back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

After a refund, the License Key is deactivated for the refunded period.

Subscriptions

You can cancel a subscription at any time. It then stays active until the end of the period you already paid for and does not renew. If you were charged for a renewal you did not want, ask within 14 days and we refund it.

Who processes refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com Market Limited, the Merchant of Record for all our orders, which issues the refund. Where Paddle’s buyer terms or the law of your country give you more favourable rights, those apply. More details are in section 7 of our Terms of use.
